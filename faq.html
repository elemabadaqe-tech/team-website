Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our team and projects.

 
 
What does your team do?

We use HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git, and GitHub to create responsive and interactive web solutions.

 
How can I contact your team?

You can reach us through the Contact page, where you'll find our contact information and message form.

Yes. We collaborate on projects using GitHub, which helps us manage code, track changes, and work efficiently as a team.
